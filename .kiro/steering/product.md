# Product principles — Afterlife BBS

Steering for every change to this project. If a proposed feature conflicts
with a principle below, the principle wins.

## 1. Slow is the product
The board is deliberately slower than a feed. Pagination, not infinite
scroll. Newest first, not "most engaging". When the posts end, the board
says so and sends the reader outside. Never add a mechanism whose purpose
is to keep people scrolling.

## 2. One small community
This board belongs to one place: a building, a dorm, a club, a class, a
town. Features must make sense at 30–300 neighbours. Nothing here should
assume virality, growth, or a global audience.

## 3. No engagement machinery — ever
No follower counts. No like counts as a score (candles are a warm
acknowledgement, capped at one per neighbour, never ranked). No ads, no ad
slots, no sponsored posts. No algorithmic recommendations, no "for you".
No streaks, no badges, no notifications engineered to pull people back.

## 4. Neighbours first
Tone: a notice board by the old oak, not a platform. Posts read like
notes pinned by hand. Help is the core interaction — the Séance Circle
exists so asking for help is normal and answering it is visible.

## 5. The board rests
00:00–06:00 local time the board sleeps. Reading is always allowed;
posting waits for morning. Rest is a feature, not a bug, and the UI must
say why instead of failing silently.

## 6. The community can leave
Export the whole board as one JSON file, any time. Import it somewhere
else. No lock-in, no account required to take your own words with you.

## 7. Honest, local, free
All data stays in the visitor's browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. No
accounts, no tracking, no paid APIs, no keys. If a feature needs a server
or a payment, it does not belong in this project.

## 8. Craft over novelty
CRT phosphor, scanlines, ASCII art, typewriter — but never at the cost of
readability. If a decoration makes the board harder to read on a phone or
with a screen reader, the decoration goes. Reduce-motion and
high-contrast are first-class, not afterthoughts.
